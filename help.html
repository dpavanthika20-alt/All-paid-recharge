Help & support

Answers to common questions about this demo platform.

Is this a real recharge service?

No. All Paid Recharge is a student/portfolio demo. Recharges, payments and transaction IDs are simulated — no real telecom operator is contacted and no money moves.

Where is my data stored?

Your account, plans and transaction history are stored only in your browser's local storage for this demo. Clearing your browser data will remove them.

Why did my demo payment fail?

The payment simulation randomly marks a small share of transactions as failed to show what a failed-payment flow looks like. No real charge was ever attempted.

Can I use my real card or UPI ID?

Please don't enter real financial details. This form never transmits data anywhere — use any demo values, like 4111 1111 1111 1111 for a card number.

How do I reset my demo account?

Open your browser's developer tools, clear local storage for this site, and refresh — a fresh demo account will be seeded automatically.
